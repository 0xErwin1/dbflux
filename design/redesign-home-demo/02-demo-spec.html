Rules

DriverOne sticky section. T = 600 × ScrollTrigger progress, and every property is a pure function of T. The window trails the scroll position by a 220 ms ease (elapsed capped at 1 s), so a fast flick still plays its steps; at rest any direction gives the same frame.Scroll length15 × 100vh of scroll (a 1500vh track with a CSS sticky stage), about 40 T per screen. No snapping and no wheel capture.EasingeaseInOutCubic for every interpolation inside T: camera, cursor travel, tree rows, row collapse, builder width. The 220 ms scroll ease sits on top and only smooths the input.SkipSkip the demo sits next to the chapter buttons. It, any in-page link that lands past the demo, and the Top button jump straight to the far edge of the track, showing the last or first frame, then scroll smoothly outside it. The demo never plays at speed.PageThe section replaces the old Keyboard, Governance and Audit sections. Keyboard stays as a strip right after the demo (#keyboard), and #governance lands on the Audit step. The drivers section follows the demo and owns #drivers. The header lists Keyboard, Drivers, Compare, Docs and About.CursorRests at a target, then travels during the last 9 T before the next one, with an 8% perpendicular arc. Press: scale 0.86 from click − 0.6 to click + 0.8. Ripple: 4 T, scale 0.3 → 1.2, opacity 0.8 → 0. Double-click = two clicks 2 T apart. Drawn in window space, so its size never follows the camera.CameraA region {cx, cy, w} in app pixels. scale = max(windowW / 1532, windowW / w); translate centres the region and clamps to the app edges. Applied as one transform on the live DOM, so text stays crisp.TypingVisible characters = round(u × length) over the typing range. Reverse scroll removes them in order.TargetsElements carry data-demo ids (where, refresh, save, rows, hdr-status, chart-toggle, rail-audit, builder-sql, audit-new). Their boxes are read once after layout and on resize, in app coordinates, so the cursor lands on the real control.ThemeThe window uses the Islands values through custom properties; the light theme swaps each value for its counterpart from the light Islands boards. Site chrome reads tokens.css.
