Rules
  • Builder and slots are one query: editing either updates the other on every change.
  • Find results are editable because they keep _id. Projection may hide fields, never _id.
  • A group stage switches to Aggregate: the result opens in the Aggregate view, read-only.
  • The field picker comes from the Schema view sample; typing a path that is not in the sample is allowed and marked unsampled.
  • Only read stages. No update or delete from the builder.
