Focus ring
  • Keyboard focus only (focus-visible), never after a pointer click.
  • Single controls: tint 1.5 px following the cut. Outside at 2 px on filled (primary, danger), inside otherwise.
  • Composite controls (segmented, chips, tabs, lists) never ring the container: the focused item gets a tint underline or bar, or the row wash.
  • Selected is fill only, no border, so rings never nest.
Motion and press
  • Hover and press change fill only, never size or position. 150 ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1).
  • Pressed shows while the pointer or the key is down; keyboard activation (Enter, Space) shows it too.
  • Reduced motion: switch instantly.
Section labels
  • Archivo Expanded 800, uppercase, 10-12 px, tracking +0.14em.
  • Where letter spacing is not available (GPUI today), keep Archivo Expanded 800 without tracking. Do not fall back to plain Archivo 700.
Muted text
  • Dark muted is #8E8996: 5.2:1 on raised, higher on panel and bg. The old #7F7A87 read 4.2:1 on raised.
  • Light muted stays #6B6572: 4.7:1 on raised.
  • Light surfaces are raised #EEEAF0, line #E3DEE6, line-2 #CBC4D1 everywhere.
