Behavior
  • Anchored popover under the bell, 440 px wide, up to 70% of the window tall. It floats over the islands and never reflows them.
  • Esc or a click outside closes it. Opening it does not mark anything read; opening a row does.
  • A row opens its target: the MCP approvals tab on that request, Audit filtered by the correlation id, the What is new dialog, or the tab that ran the task.
  • Approvals are reviewed in the approvals tab, never approved from the popover, because the preview and policy live there.
  • The status-bar approvals pill stays. The update pill leaves the status bar; updates live here and in the bell badge.
  • Only user-facing errors already reported through the error seam appear. Each keeps its correlation id.
