Accent color per theme (#987): where it applies, and readability at the extremes

One accent per theme drives primary buttons, checked boxes, the progress bar, the text cursor, the selection, the focus ring and selected rows. Hover and pressed are a little lighter and darker. The text tint is lightened on Dark (darkened on Light) until it reaches 4.5:1 on the pane, primary button text turns near-black when white would fall under 4.5:1, and the fills (buttons, checked boxes, the progress bar, the focus ring, the cursor and the selection) are lightened on Dark (darkened on Light) until they reach 3:1 against the pane, so an accent close to the background never makes them vanish. The palette's own accent is left exactly as it is today. Syntax colors, including keywords and the schema tree, do not move.
